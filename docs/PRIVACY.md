# Clink privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Clink is made by Samlytics Data Services LTD ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information you put into the Clink app.

## The short version

Clink keeps everything on your phone. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your information. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

## What Clink stores on your phone

To work, Clink saves the following in a database on your own device:

- your children's names, colours and photos
- goals, activities, rewards, gifts and notes you enter
- short voice messages you record for celebrations
- your settings, such as currency and reminders

This information never leaves your phone unless you choose to send it, as described below.

## Permissions Clink asks for

- **Camera**: to take photos of your child or a reached goal, and to scan the pairing code on your partner's phone.
- **Photos and media**: to choose a photo, and to save goal photos to a "Clink" album in your gallery.
- **Microphone**: to record a short message that plays during your children's celebrations. Recording only happens when you press record.
- **Notifications**: to show reminders that are scheduled on your phone. No notifications come from a server.
- **Local network**: to find and sync with your partner's phone on your home Wi-Fi.

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

## When information leaves your phone

Only when you choose to:

- **Two-parent sync.** If you pair two phones, Clink sends your family's records directly between them over your home Wi-Fi. It does not go over the internet or through our servers. Every message is encrypted with a key that only your paired phones hold.
- **Backup and sharing.** If you save a backup or share a weekly report, Clink hands the file or text to the app you pick, such as Google Drive, email or a messaging app. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

## Children

Clink is designed for parents to use. Children may look at the screen with a parent, but the app is not intended for children to use alone. We do not collect any information from children, or from anyone else.

## Deleting your information

Uninstalling Clink, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes everything it stored. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
